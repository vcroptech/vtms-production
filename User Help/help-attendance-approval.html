Reviewing a Cancelled / All Classes Cancelled record

These rows land in your queue specifically because no formal Class Cancellation record exists for them yet — the teacher or school reported the cancellation, but you haven't logged it yourself. Open the record and ask:

What you seeDecisionNotes
Matches a cancellation you know actually happened (school event, weather, etc.)ApproveConsider also raising the formal Class Cancellation yourself for future record-keeping — it won't affect this record either way.
You don't recognize this cancellation, or it doesn't match anything you're aware ofReject and ask, or contact vCrop AdminAsk the teacher what happened. Don't approve on a guess — this still affects billing as an "Unrecorded Cancellation".
Topics/Remark doesn't explain why the class was cancelledReject, with a reasonAsk for a specific reason, same as you would for a vague Conducted entry.
ⓘUnlike a Conducted class, whether a Cancelled / All Classes Cancelled record is billable does not depend on your approve/reject decision — that's already determined by whether a formal Class Cancellation record exists and how much advance notice it gave. Your review here is about verifying the record is genuine, not about billing.
