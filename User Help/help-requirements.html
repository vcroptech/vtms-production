The full pipeline
1. Raised
WhoHead Teacher or Cluster Coordinator. WhatFills in School, Grade, Subject, Number of teachers needed, Classes per day, Syllabus status (mandatory) and optional notes.
→
2. CC approval
WhoThe school's Cluster Coordinator, only if one is assigned and they didn't raise it themselves. WhatSkipped entirely if there's no CC for that school, or if the CC is the one who raised it — goes straight to Org Admin instead.
→
3. Org approval
WhoOrg Admin or vCrop Admin. WhatApprove moves it to Resumes. Reject requires a mandatory reason and ends the requirement.
→
4. Resumes
WhovCrop Admin uploads candidates; Org Admin, CC and Head Teacher review, shortlist, select and reject. WhatSee the Resumes sub-stages below — this is the longest part of the process.
→
5. Allocation
WhoWhoever allocates the teacher (Teacher Allocation screen). WhatPicking the final-selected requirement auto-fills school and classes/day. Completing the allocation marks the requirement Completed.
ⓘThe requirement's status badge shows Approved (Shortlisting) for the entire Resumes stage — it doesn't change again until a teacher is actually allocated against it, which is what moves it to Completed. All the shortlisting/selecting activity happens on the individual candidate resumes, not on the requirement's own status.
Resumes sub-stages — one candidate's journey
Uploaded
WhovCrop Admin only. WhatCandidate name + a Google Drive/OneDrive link shared as "Anyone with the link", plus optional notes.
→
Shortlisted
WhoOrg Admin, CC and Head Teacher each have their own independent, one-way shortlist slot. WhatAny one of them shortlisting is enough to move the candidate into "Shortlisted for interview" — the other two slots stay open and are tracked separately in the audit trail.
→
Selected (school)
WhoCC or Head Teacher only. WhatRequires the candidate to already be shortlisted. Marks them as the school's pick, awaiting Org Admin's final sign-off.
→
⭐ Final Selected
WhoOrg Admin only. WhatRequires the candidate to already be Selected (school) first — Org Admin cannot skip straight to Final Select. This is the candidate that becomes eligible for teacher allocation.
⚠Reject is available to Org Admin, CC and Head Teacher at any point before a candidate is Final Selected — it always requires a reason, and is final for that candidate.
