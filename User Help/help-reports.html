The six report tabs — who sees what, and why
TabWho sees itUse it for
AttendanceEveryoneEvery individual attendance record, with HT Approval Status — the most granular view.
Class CancellationsOrg Admin, vCrop AdminEvery cancellation sent, with advance notice timing.
Allocated TeachersOrg Admin, vCrop AdminCurrent snapshot of who's allocated where — no date filter, since it's "as of now."
RequirementsOrg Admin, vCrop AdminRequirement fulfillment tracking, by status.
Summary RollupsOrg Admin, vCrop AdminAggregated totals, either Per School or Per Teacher.
Consolidated (Billing)Org Admin, vCrop Admin, and Teacher (their own data only)The actual billing output — Total Billable Minutes per teacher/class/subject.
ⓘCluster Coordinator and Head Teacher only ever see the Attendance tab. Teachers additionally see Consolidated (Billing) — scoped to just their own records — for transparency into their own billing picture.
Filters available
FilterWhere it applies
OrganizationvCrop Admin only, and only shown when there's more than one organization.
SchoolAny role with more than one school in scope.
SubjectEvery tab.
StatusAttendance (Fully Approved / Pending / Rejected), Requirements (full status list), Allocations (Active / De-allocated) — each tab has its own options.
Billing monthConsolidated (Billing) only — a quick alternative to picking a custom date range.
From / To dateEvery tab except Allocated Teachers (a current-state snapshot has no date dimension).
⚠On the Consolidated tab, if both a Billing month and a custom From/To range are set, the date range always takes priority over the billing month.
Download limits — why your export might be rejected
RoleMaximum download range
Org Admin, vCrop Admin92 days (about one quarter)
Everyone else (Teacher, CC, Head Teacher)31 days (about one month)

This cap exists purely to avoid overloading the server on a single export — it applies to Attendance, Class Cancellations, Summary Rollups, and Consolidated (when using a custom date range). Allocated Teachers has no date filter at all, so the cap doesn't apply there. If your range is rejected, narrow it and try again.

The Consolidated (Billing) report — how the columns add up
ColumnMeaning
Approved Min.Conducted classes that Head Teacher has approved.
Short-Notice Cancelled Min.Formal cancellation record, notice under 24 hours — billable.
Unrecorded Cancellation Min.Cancelled / All Classes Cancelled with no formal record at all — billable regardless of approval status.
Total Billable Min.Approved + Short-Notice Cancelled + Unrecorded Cancellation — every billable minute, traceable to its source.
Un-Approved Min.Conducted classes not yet approved by Head Teacher — shown for reference only, not yet billable.
ⓘHover any Short-Notice or Unrecorded cell in the on-screen table to see exactly which dates contribute to that number. The Session (e.g. "2026-27") and Month/Year are derived automatically from an April–March academic year.
