Who can raise an issue — and about what

👩‍🏫 Teacher raises

About their own school — a problem affecting their teaching there (timetable clash, missing books, calendar conflict, etc.).

Picks one of their own active school allocations, a category, and a description.

🏫 Head Teacher raises

About a specific teacher at their school — a problem with or concerning that teacher.

Picks any currently-allocated teacher at their own school(s), a category, and a description.

ⓘOnly Teacher and Head Teacher can raise an issue. Cluster Coordinator, Org Admin and vCrop Admin can view and act on issues, but never raise one themselves.

The one thing that isn't obvious: who can act depends on who raised it

Action rights are not based on the usual role hierarchy — they depend entirely on who raised the specific issue you're looking at.

Issue raised by...Who can click In Progress / ResolveWho cannot act
Head Teacher (about a teacher)The subject Teacher, and vCrop AdminOrg Admin, Cluster Coordinator, and the raising Head Teacher themselves
Teacher (about their school)Org Admin, Cluster Coordinator, Head Teacher, and vCrop AdminThe raising Teacher themselves
⚠vCrop Admin can always act on any issue, regardless of who raised it. Everyone else's ability to act flips entirely based on the raiser — the same person (say, a Head Teacher) can act on issues teachers raise, but is locked out of acting on issues they raised themselves.
